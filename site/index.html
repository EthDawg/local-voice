Voice 1.3.0

Download Voice ZIP ↓
  1. Unzip, then move Workbench Voice.app to your Applications folder. Quit an older copy before replacing it.
  2. Open it and stay online while the English speech model prepares. This can take several minutes; the download is larger than the app itself.
  3. Find the waveform in the menu bar. Start a short dictation and allow Microphone access when macOS asks. Copy-to-clipboard works without Accessibility access.

Automatic paste is optional. Enable Accessibility only if you want Voice to insert text into another app. It never presses Return.

Try Shortcuts dictation · early access

Action discovery and audio connections are verified. The complete live recording workflow still needs tester confirmation.

In Apple Shortcuts, add Record Audio, then Transcribe with Workbench, then Create Note, Copy to Clipboard, or another action that accepts text. Speak and stop the recording in Shortcuts. Voice transcribes it locally and passes your words to the next action.

Optional online reading with Speko

Mac voices work free and offline. For Speko, choose Gateway + Router during account setup, create an API key, then save it in Voice’s Read aloud → Speko · online controls. The key stays in macOS Keychain. Selected reading text goes to Speko and its voice provider; usage may be billed. Privacy details.

StageMark 1.2.1

Download StageMark ZIP ↓
  1. Unzip, then move Workbench StageMark.app to your Applications folder. Quit an older copy before replacing it.
  2. Open it. Find the pencil in the menu bar and open the Draw controls.
  3. Hold ⌃ ⌥ D to draw; release to return to your app. Press ⌃ ⌥ X to clear the ink.

For Zoom, Teams or Meet, share your entire display so others can see the annotations. Window-only sharing can exclude them.

Opening your download

Download from the linked EthDawg GitHub releases. These builds are signed with Ethan Harley’s Developer ID and include Apple’s notarisation ticket.

macOS may ask you to confirm opening an app downloaded from the internet. Voice separately asks for Microphone access when you start dictation; Accessibility is optional for automatic paste.

If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, the app is damaged, or it will harm your Mac, stop and report the exact message and version. Use Apple’s opening guidance; don’t disable Gatekeeper. A managed work Mac may need your IT team’s approval.

These are early-access releases. New-Mac installation and older macOS versions still need independent tester coverage. App Store availability will be linked here after Apple approves and publishes a store edition.

Updates, data and uninstalling

There is no automatic updater yet. Download a newer release, quit the app, and replace the copy in Applications. Keep one installed copy. Existing history, boards and settings live outside the app and survive replacement.

To uninstall, quit and remove the app from Applications. Voice history stays in ~/Library/Application Support/LocalVoice; StageMark boards stay in ~/Library/Application Support/StageMark. Remove that data separately only if you want to erase it.

Voice processes audio locally after its initial model download. StageMark makes no network requests. Neither app uploads your transcripts or boards. This website has no analytics or feedback database; the hosting provider still receives normal page requests.
