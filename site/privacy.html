Your work stays
on your Mac.

Workbench Voice and StageMark are free, open-source Mac apps published by Ethan Harley. Neither app includes advertising, analytics or an account system.

StageMark

Annotations, saved boards and preferences are stored locally on your Mac. StageMark draws overlay windows; it does not record your screen, upload your boards or send usage information to us. If you share your screen using another app, that app controls what is shared and how it is handled.

Voice

Dictation audio is processed on your Mac. Drafts, recent transcripts, dictionary entries and preferences are stored locally. Voice uses the microphone when you start recording. Optional Accessibility access lets it insert text into the app you are using.

The initial speech model download requires an internet connection to the model provider. That provider receives normal download-request information, such as your IP address. Your dictation audio and transcripts are not uploaded for transcription. Exported audio is saved where you choose.

This website and feedback

This website has no analytics, advertising or feedback database. The hosting provider, Vercel, receives normal page requests to serve the site. Trial progress and report drafts remain in page memory and are lost when you close or reload the page.

Using “Review on GitHub” sends your draft to GitHub for you to review. Posting an issue or discussion makes its contents public. Copying a report for a coding agent lets you choose where to send it. Remove private text, recordings and identifying details before sharing feedback.

Your choices and questions

You can control Microphone and Accessibility permissions in macOS System Settings. The apps store their working data locally; we cannot access or remotely delete it. You control any copies you export or share.

For questions, open a general issue in Voice or StageMark. For security-sensitive information, use the repository’s private vulnerability reporting option instead of a public issue.
