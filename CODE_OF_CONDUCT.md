# Community expectations

Workbench welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Help newcomers, assume good intent, give specific feedback on the work, and respect another person's time and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and repeated personal attacks are not welcome. Do not post someone else's private information or contact them after they ask you to stop.

These expectations apply to this project's issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other project spaces. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, lock a conversation, or restrict participation, using a response proportionate to the behavior. Mistakes and constructive disagreement are welcome; abuse is not.

For a conduct concern, use GitHub's **Report content** menu on the relevant item; do not repeat private or abusive material in a public issue. You can also block an account through GitHub. Public technical disagreements can be raised respectfully in the original thread for maintainer review.
