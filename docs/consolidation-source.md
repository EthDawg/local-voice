# Source provenance

Workbench is the continuing project at [EthDawg/workbench](https://github.com/EthDawg/workbench), developed on `main`. The repository was renamed from `local-voice` in place, preserving its history, issues, PRs and releases. The separate [StageMark repository](https://github.com/EthDawg/StageMark) is retained as a read-only archive.

StageKit was imported at `8b6a24a` from the StageMark `feature/demo-preview` checkout on 12 September 2026, including its uncommitted presenter polish. StageMark HEAD was `e7022b7`; Voice base was `1a68b50`. A 14 September audit confirmed that the dirty presenter files are represented by that import or intentional host integration changes. Native modules now share one process; source preservation alone does not prove release or hardware acceptance.

Historical refs are preserved under `archive/` tags before retiring their development branches. Original local source and unfinished work are retained. The internal `LocalVoice` and `StageKit` module names and legacy data locations remain for compatibility; they are not separate active projects.
